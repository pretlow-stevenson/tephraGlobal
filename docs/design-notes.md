Original generation prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: photographic landscape artwork for the Tephra Global holding-company website, a sophisticated minimal white website.
Primary request: a white volcanic landscape, a snow-covered volcanic crater and pale ash ridges, evocative of actual tephra and snowy mountain peaks.
Scene: an imagined volcanic highland seen from a slightly elevated distant viewpoint, one unmistakable broad crater-shaped volcanic mountain in the center-right with finely eroded slopes of pale pumice, ash and fresh snow, smaller distant snow peaks behind in atmospheric mist. Thin subtle veil of white volcanic steam rising from the crater. No dramatic explosion.
Composition: very wide panoramic 3:2 image, visually sparse upper half with a nearly white overcast sky; main crater centered at 64% across frame, mid-ground peak at about 48% down. Foreground consists of delicate sinuous ash and snow ridges, quiet and spacious. Leftmost quarter fades into soft almost white cloud and empty sky for blending at edge; bottom and right edges softly pale.
Style: fine-art landscape photography, tactile geological detail, very high-key white-on-white monochrome, subtle cool gray shadow definition. Must feel like a real landscape photographed on an overcast winter day, not a 3D render, paper sculpture, polygon illustration, or stock extreme adventure photo. Rich detail within restrained tonal range, muted charcoal only in a few narrow exposed seams.
Lighting: diffuse luminous winter daylight, soft shadows, serene and expansive.
Palette: white, off-white, light silver-gray, very small amounts of medium cool gray. No blue sky, no saturated colors, no orange lava, no black background.
No typography, text, people, buildings, roads, logos, frames, or watermarks. This is artwork only, not a website mockup.
```
